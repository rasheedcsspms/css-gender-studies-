## MCQ
Gender schema theory (1981), which holds that children sort the world through mental maps of "masculine" and "feminine", was proposed by: || Sandra Bem | Lawrence Kohlberg | Albert Bandura | None of these || A || Sandra Bem (1981); Kohlberg proposed cognitive-developmental stages, Bandura social learning.
In Kohlberg's theory, the stage at which a child understands that gender does not change with clothes or activities is: || Gender identity | Gender stability | Gender constancy | None of these || C || Gender constancy (about age 6–7), after identity (2–3) and stability (4–5).
"Heteronormativity" refers to: || The assumption that heterosexuality is the only normal sexuality | The study of hormones | The equal treatment of all sexualities | None of these || A || A key concept of queer theory: the assumption of two opposite genders and that only heterosexuality is normal.
The Supreme Court's 2009 order recognising a third gender in CNICs came in a petition filed by: || Asma Jahangir | Hina Jilani | Benazir Bhutto | None of these || D || None of these — Dr Muhammad Aslam Khaki (*Khaki v. SSP (Operations) Rawalpindi*).
*Sexual Politics* (1970), a founding text of radical feminism, was written by: || Kate Millett | Shulamith Firestone | Germaine Greer | None of these || A || Kate Millett (1970); Firestone wrote *The Dialectic of Sex* (1970).
Engels' *The Origin of the Family, Private Property and the State* (1884) linked women's subordination to: || Biology | The rise of private property | Religion | None of these || B || Engels called the overthrow of mother-right, with private property, "the world historical defeat of the female sex".
"Dual-systems theory" holds that women's oppression results from: || Capitalism alone | Patriarchy alone | Capitalism and patriarchy together | None of these || C || Heidi Hartmann, "The Unhappy Marriage of Marxism and Feminism" (1979): two linked systems.
The term "ecofeminism" was coined in 1974 by: || Vandana Shiva | Françoise d'Eaubonne | Carolyn Merchant | Bina Agarwal || B || Françoise d'Eaubonne (1974); Bina Agarwal later proposed feminist environmentalism (1992).
The UN Decade for Women ran from: || 1975–1985 | 1976–1985 | 1980–1990 | None of these || B || 1976–1985, after International Women's Year (1975), with the themes Equality, Development and Peace.
UN Security Council Resolution 1325 (2000) concerns: || Women, peace and security | Child marriage | Maternal health | None of these || A || Resolution 1325 (31 October 2000) calls for women's participation in peace-making and protection in conflict.
The first Aurat March was held in: || 2016 | 2017 | 2018 | 2019 || C || 8 March 2018, first in Karachi.
The distinction between women's "practical" and "strategic" gender interests (1985) was first drawn by: || Maxine Molyneux | Ester Boserup | Diane Elson | None of these || A || Maxine Molyneux, "Mobilization without Emancipation?" (1985); Caroline Moser turned it into practical and strategic gender needs.
W. W. Rostow's *The Stages of Economic Growth* (1960) is the classic text of: || Dependency theory | World-system theory | Structural functionalism | None of these || D || None of these — modernisation theory (five stages from traditional society to high mass consumption).
Which SDG target calls for recognising and valuing unpaid care and domestic work? || 5.2 | 5.3 | 5.4 | 5.5 || C || Target 5.4 (5.2 violence; 5.3 child marriage; 5.5 leadership).
Female labour-force participation in Pakistan in the Labour Force Survey 2024–25 was: || 14.5% | 21.4% | 33.8% | None of these || D || None of these — 24.4% (21.4% in 2020–21; 14.5% is Balochistan's rate).
Pakistan's Lady Health Worker Programme was launched in: || 1988 | 1994 | 2002 | 2008 || B || 1994, under Benazir Bhutto.
The "critical mass" of women needed to change the culture of a legislature is usually put at about: || 10% | 17% | 30% | 50% || C || About 30% (Dahlerup, 1988; ECOSOC target, 1990).
Pakistan's first woman Foreign Minister (2011) was: || Tehmina Janjua | Hina Rabbani Khar | Sherry Rehman | Maleeha Lodhi || B || Hina Rabbani Khar (2011); Tehmina Janjua was the first woman Foreign Secretary (2017).
Sharmeen Obaid-Chinoy's Oscar-winning documentary *Saving Face* (2012) dealt with: || Honour killing | Child marriage | Girls' education | None of these || D || None of these — acid attacks; *A Girl in the River* (2016) dealt with honour killing.
Section 2(r) of the Punjab Protection of Women against Violence Act 2016 recognises all of the following as violence EXCEPT: || Economic abuse | Stalking | Cybercrime | None of these || D || None of these — all three are listed, with domestic and sexual violence, psychological abuse and abetment.
