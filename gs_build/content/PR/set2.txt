## MCQ
Thomas Laqueur's "one-sex model" holds that before the eighteenth century: || Women were seen as a separate species | Women were seen as inverted, lesser versions of men | Sex was considered irrelevant to social roles | None of these || B || In *Making Sex* (1990) Laqueur argued that Europe saw the female body as an inverted male body until a "two-sex" model emerged in the 18th century.
Barbara Risman (2004) described gender as: || A performance without an actor | A social structure working at individual, interactional and institutional levels | A hormonal trait | None of these || B || Risman: gender as a social structure at three levels — the individual, everyday interaction and institutions.
In Berger and Luckmann's *The Social Construction of Reality* (1966), the stage at which human-made arrangements come to look like solid facts is called: || Objectivation | Externalisation | Internalisation | None of these || A || Externalisation (people create arrangements) → objectivation (they look like facts) → internalisation (new generations learn them as natural).
Which of the following is a "marked" term? || Doctor | Lady doctor | Chairperson | Teacher || B || A marked term singles a woman out as the exception to a male norm ("lady doctor").
Which of the following is NOT one of Sylvia Walby's six structures of patriarchy (1990)? || Paid work | The state | Religion | Male violence || C || Walby's six: paid work, household production, culture, sexuality, violence and the state.
"Under Western Eyes" (1984), a critique of Western feminist portrayals of "Third World women", was written by: || Gayatri Chakravorty Spivak | Uma Narayan | Chandra Talpade Mohanty | None of these || C || Chandra Talpade Mohanty (1984).
The "patriarchal dividend" — the advantage all men gain from women's subordination — is a concept of: || R. W. Connell | Michael Kimmel | Michael Kaufman | None of these || A || R. W. Connell, *Masculinities* (1995).
*Qur'an and Woman* (1992), a founding text of Islamic feminism, was written by: || Fatima Mernissi | Asma Barlas | Riffat Hassan | None of these || D || None of these — Amina Wadud (1992). Mernissi wrote *The Veil and the Male Elite* (1987).
The Optional Protocol to CEDAW (1999): || Allows individual complaints to the CEDAW Committee | Has been ratified by Pakistan | Created UN Women | None of these || A || It allows individual complaints and inquiries; Pakistan is not a party.
Pakistan's reservation to CEDAW concerns: || Article 2 | Article 16 | Article 29(1) | None of these || C || A reservation to Article 29(1) (disputes to the International Court of Justice), plus a general declaration subject to the Constitution.
The Sindhiani Tehreek, a mass movement of rural Sindhi women, began in: || 1975 | 1979 | 1981 | 1982 || D || 1982, joining women's rights to struggles over land and democracy.
Diane Elson argued that structural adjustment programmes treat women's labour as: || Infinitely elastic | Fully remunerated | Highly mechanised | None of these || A || *Male Bias in the Development Process* (1991): SAPs assume women's unpaid work can stretch without limit to absorb cuts.
In Wallerstein's world-system theory, the zone that both exploits the periphery and is exploited by the core is: || The hinterland | The semi-periphery | The metropole | None of these || B || The semi-periphery (core, semi-periphery, periphery — Wallerstein, 1974).
Naila Kabeer (1999) defined empowerment through three interrelated dimensions: || Access, control and participation | Welfare, equity and efficiency | Resources, agency and achievements | None of these || C || Resources, agency and achievements — the ability to make strategic life choices previously denied.
The concept of "global care chains" (2000) was developed by: || Arlie Hochschild | Saskia Sassen | Guy Standing | Maria Mies || A || Arlie Hochschild: poor women migrate to care for richer families' children, leaving their own children with others.
Under the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961, a *talaq* takes effect: || Immediately on pronouncement | On a decree of the Family Court | On the Arbitration Council's permission | None of these || D || None of these — 90 days after written notice to the chairman of the Union Council.
A wife's right to *khula* was recognised by the superior courts in: || The Saima Waheed case (2004) | The Safia Bibi case (1983) | The Shah Bano case (1985) | None of these || D || None of these — *Khurshid Bibi v. Muhammad Amin* (1967). Saima Waheed concerned marriage without a wali.
In Hanna Pitkin's typology, representatives who act in the interests of those they represent illustrate which type of representation? || Formal | Descriptive | Symbolic | None of these || D || None of these — substantive representation (Pitkin, 1967).
The World Bank's 1989 report that spoke of a "crisis of governance" concerned: || South Asia | Latin America | Sub-Saharan Africa | Eastern Europe || C || Sub-Saharan Africa (1989); the Bank defined governance in 1992.
Johan Galtung's "cultural violence" (1990) refers to: || Violence committed by cultural groups | Aspects of culture that legitimise direct and structural violence | Violence portrayed in films | None of these || B || Cultural violence: beliefs and symbols that make direct and structural violence look right or acceptable.
