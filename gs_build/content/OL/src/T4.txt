Q: The "development era" is often dated from Truman's "Point Four" speech of:
A: 1945 | 20 January 1949 | 1955 | 1960
K: B | 20 January 1949.

Q: Mahbub ul Haq defined development as:
A: Rising GDP | Enlarging people's choices | Trickle-down growth | Industrialisation
K: B | "Enlarging people's choices" — UNDP Human Development Report (1990).

Q: *Development as Freedom* (1999) was written by:
A: Mahbub ul Haq | Amartya Sen | Martha Nussbaum | Naila Kabeer
K: B | Amartya Sen.

Q: *If Women Counted* (1988), on women's unpaid work missing from national accounts, was written by:
A: Diane Elson | Marilyn Waring | Ester Boserup | Maria Mies
K: B | Marilyn Waring.

Q: The UNDP's Gender Inequality Index was introduced in:
A: 1990 | 1995 | 2006 | 2010
K: D | 2010 (GDI and GEM in 1995).

Q: The World Development Report 2012 called gender equality:
A: A human right only | "Smart economics" | A cultural question | None of these
K: B | "Smart economics" — criticised by Chant and Sweetman (2012).

Q: Gandhi called Katherine Mayo's *Mother India* (1927):
A: "A drain inspector's report" | "A civilising mission" | "A true mirror" | None of these
K: A | "A drain inspector's report".

Q: The Punjab Laws Act of 1872 hurt Muslim women's inheritance by making which the first rule?
A: Islamic law | Custom | English law | Hindu law
K: B | Custom.

Q: Maria Mies' study of housewifisation focused on the lace makers of:
A: Sialkot | Narsapur | Dhaka | Faisalabad
K: B | Narsapur, India.

Q: *Caliban and the Witch* (2004), linking early capitalism to the witch-hunts, was written by:
A: Maria Mies | Silvia Federici | Vandana Shiva | Diane Elson
K: B | Silvia Federici.

Q: The distinction between colonialism and "coloniality of power" (2000) was drawn by:
A: Aníbal Quijano | Arturo Escobar | María Lugones | Wolfgang Sachs
K: A | Aníbal Quijano.

Q: *Encountering Development* (1995), a post-development critique, was written by:
A: Wolfgang Sachs | Andre Gunder Frank | Walter Rodney | None of these
K: D | None of these — Arturo Escobar.

Q: Rostow's stage that comes after "preconditions for take-off" is:
A: Traditional society | Take-off | Drive to maturity | High mass consumption
K: B | Take-off.

Q: *The Passing of Traditional Society* (1958) was written by:
A: Daniel Lerner | W. W. Rostow | Inkeles and Smith | Talcott Parsons
K: A | Daniel Lerner.

Q: Boserup contrasted "female farming systems" based on the hoe with "male farming systems" based on the:
A: Tractor | Plough | Irrigation | Sickle
K: B | Plough.

Q: "The development of underdevelopment" (1966) was coined by:
A: Raúl Prebisch | Andre Gunder Frank | Walter Rodney | Fernando Cardoso
K: B | Andre Gunder Frank.

Q: *How Europe Underdeveloped Africa* (1972) was written by:
A: Walter Rodney | Samir Amin | Frantz Fanon | None of these
K: A | Walter Rodney.

Q: The "global assembly line" (1983) was described by:
A: Fuentes and Ehrenreich | Smith and Wallerstein | Chant and Sweetman | None of these
K: A | Annette Fuentes and Barbara Ehrenreich.

Q: George Murdock (1949) named four functions of the family. Which is NOT one of them?
A: Sexual | Reproductive | Political | Educational
K: C | Sexual, reproductive, economic and educational.

Q: In Parsons and Bales (1955), women play the:
A: Instrumental role | Expressive role | Latent role | Productive role
K: B | Expressive role; men the instrumental role.

Q: The US Percy Amendment (1973) required:
A: Equal pay | US aid to integrate women | Abortion rights | Quotas in Congress
K: B | US aid to integrate women into development.

Q: The IDS workshop on "The Continuing Subordination of Women" (1978) is linked to:
A: WID | WAD | GAD | None of these
K: B | Women and Development (WAD).

Q: Moser's triple role does NOT include:
A: Productive work | Reproductive work | Community-managing work | Military work
K: D | Productive, reproductive and community-managing.

Q: Naila Kabeer (1999) defined empowerment through three dimensions:
A: Access, control, participation | Resources, agency, achievements | Welfare, equity, efficiency | None of these
K: B | Resources, agency and achievements.

Q: Sara Longwe's framework ends at the highest level of:
A: Welfare | Access | Participation | Control
K: D | Welfare, access, conscientisation, participation, control.

Q: The term "Washington Consensus" (1989) was coined by:
A: John Williamson | Joseph Stiglitz | Diane Elson | Milton Friedman
K: A | John Williamson.

Q: UNICEF's *Adjustment with a Human Face* was published in:
A: 1982 | 1987 | 1991 | 1999
K: B | 1987.

Q: Diane Elson argued that structural adjustment treats women's labour as:
A: "Infinitely elastic" | "Highly productive" | "Fully paid" | None of these
K: A | "Infinitely elastic" (*Male Bias in the Development Process*, 1991).

Q: The feminisation of labour (1989) was described by:
A: Guy Standing | Arlie Hochschild | Saskia Sassen | Anthony Giddens
K: A | Guy Standing.

Q: The Atlanta Agreement (1997), which moved football stitching into centres, concerned:
A: Faisalabad | Sialkot | Karachi | Narsapur
K: B | Sialkot.

Q: The UNFCCC's Lima Work Programme on Gender was adopted in:
A: 2010 | 2014 | 2017 | 2022
K: B | 2014; the Gender Action Plan followed in 2017.

Q: SDG target 5.4 concerns:
A: Violence against women | Recognising and valuing unpaid care work | Child marriage | Women in leadership
K: B | Unpaid care and domestic work.
