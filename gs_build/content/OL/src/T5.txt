Q: The World Economic Forum's Global Gender Gap Index began in:
A: 1995 | 2006 | 2010 | 2015
K: B | 2006, when Pakistan ranked 112th of 115.

Q: Which of the following is NOT a sub-index of the Global Gender Gap Index?
A: Economic participation | Health and survival | Political empowerment | Access to justice
K: D | The four: economic participation, educational attainment, health and survival, political empowerment.

Q: In the Global Gender Gap Report 2017 Pakistan ranked:
A: 112th of 115 | 143rd of 144 | 148th of 148 | None of these
K: B | 143rd of 144 — "second-worst".

Q: In the Global Gender Gap Report 2025 Pakistan ranked:
A: 142nd of 146 | 143rd of 145 | 148th of 148 | 145th of 148
K: C | Last, 148th of 148, with 56.7% of the gap closed.

Q: On which sub-index did Pakistan rank lowest in 2026?
A: Economic participation | Educational attainment | Political empowerment | None of these
K: A | 144th on economic participation (130th education, 107th political empowerment).

Q: Pakistan's maternal mortality ratio in the Pakistan Maternal Mortality Survey 2019 was:
A: 70 | 140 | 186 | 276
K: C | 186 per 100,000 live births.

Q: SDG target 3.1 aims to bring the global maternal mortality ratio below:
A: 50 | 70 | 100 | 186
K: B | Below 70 per 100,000 live births by 2030.

Q: "Why did Mrs X die?" was asked at the Safe Motherhood Conference, Nairobi (1987), by:
A: Amartya Sen | Mahmoud Fathalla | Thaddeus and Maine | Mahbub ul Haq
K: B | Mahmoud Fathalla.

Q: The "three delays" model of maternal death was described by:
A: Thaddeus and Maine | Chant and Sweetman | Childs and Krook | None of these
K: A | Sereen Thaddeus and Deborah Maine (1994).

Q: The three delays are in deciding to seek care, reaching care and:
A: Paying for care | Receiving care | Registering the birth | None of these
K: B | Receiving adequate care at the facility.

Q: About what share of Pakistani women of reproductive age were anaemic (NNS 2018)?
A: 22% | 32% | 42% | 62%
K: C | About 42%.

Q: The Lady Health Worker Programme began in 1994 under:
A: Nawaz Sharif | Benazir Bhutto | Pervez Musharraf | Zia-ul-Haq
K: B | Benazir Bhutto.

Q: After the 18th Amendment (2010), health became:
A: A federal subject | A provincial subject | A concurrent subject | None of these
K: B | A provincial subject.

Q: Article 25A requires free and compulsory education for children aged:
A: 3 to 14 | 5 to 16 | 6 to 18 | 5 to 18
K: B | Ages 5 to 16 (18th Amendment, 2010).

Q: Pakistan's literacy rate in the Census 2023 was about:
A: 48% | 58% | 60.65% | 68%
K: C | 60.65% — about 68% for men and 53% for women.

Q: The province with the lowest female literacy in Pakistan is:
A: Sindh | Khyber Pakhtunkhwa | Balochistan | Punjab
K: C | Balochistan.

Q: Female labour-force participation in Pakistan in the Labour Force Survey 2024–25 was:
A: 14.5% | 21.4% | 24.4% | 33.8%
K: C | 24.4% (21.4% in 2020–21).

Q: The "second shift" (1989) — women's unpaid work after paid work — was named by:
A: Arlie Hochschild | Diane Elson | Guy Standing | Marilyn Waring
K: A | Arlie Hochschild.

Q: The first Home-Based Workers Act in Pakistan was passed in 2018 by:
A: Punjab | Khyber Pakhtunkhwa | Balochistan | None of these
K: D | None of these — Sindh (2018); Punjab passed a Domestic Workers Act in 2019.

Q: The federal Maternity and Paternity Leave Act was passed in:
A: 2010 | 2016 | 2020 | 2022
K: C | 2020.

Q: Which Article of the Constitution calls for the full participation of women in all spheres of national life?
A: Article 25 | Article 32 | Article 34 | Article 35
K: C | Article 34 (Art. 25 equality; Art. 32 local government; Art. 35 family).

Q: Which is correct about the Constitution of 1973?
A: Article 25 allows special provision for women | Article 34 calls for women's full participation in national life | Both A and B | None of these
K: C | Both: Art. 25(3) allows special provision; Art. 34 requires full participation.

Q: Under the MFLO 1961, a second marriage requires the permission of the:
A: Family Court | Arbitration Council | Union Council chairman alone | None of these
K: B | The Arbitration Council.

Q: Under the MFLO, a *talaq* takes effect after notice to the Union Council of:
A: 30 days | 60 days | 90 days | 120 days
K: C | 90 days.

Q: A wife's right to *khula* was recognised in:
A: *Saima Waheed* (2004) | *Khurshid Bibi v. Muhammad Amin* (1967) | *Shah Bano* (1985) | None of these
K: B | *Khurshid Bibi v. Muhammad Amin* (1967).

Q: The Dissolution of Muslim Marriages Act was passed in:
A: 1929 | 1939 | 1961 | 1964
K: B | 1939.

Q: The first law in Pakistan to set 18 as the minimum marriage age for girls was passed by:
A: Punjab, 2015 | Sindh, 2013 | Islamabad, 2025 | None of these
K: B | Sindh Child Marriages Restraint Act 2013; Islamabad followed in 2025.

Q: The Prevention of Anti-Women Practices Act 2011 criminalised all of the following EXCEPT:
A: Forced marriage | *Vani*/*swara* | Denial of inheritance | Dowry
K: D | Dowry is governed by the Dowry and Bridal Gifts (Restriction) Act 1976.

Q: The Enforcement of Women's Property Rights Act (Islamabad) was passed in:
A: 2011 | 2016 | 2020 | 2021
K: C | 2020.

Q: Pakistan's mobile-internet gender gap (GSMA, 2024–25) fell from 38% to:
A: 8% | 25% | 30% | None of these
K: B | 25%.

Q: The term "matrix of domination" (1990) was introduced by:
A: Kimberlé Crenshaw | Patricia Hill Collins | bell hooks | Audre Lorde
K: B | Patricia Hill Collins.

Q: Pakistan ratified the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities in:
A: 1981 | 2008 | 2011 | 2018
K: C | 2011.

Q: The Benazir Income Support Programme (2008) pays cash to:
A: Male heads of household | Women | Children directly | None of these
K: B | Women.
