Q: The legal doctrine under which a married woman's identity was absorbed into her husband's was called:
A: Coverture | Primogeniture | Suffrage | Guardianship
K: A | Coverture.

Q: The Seneca Falls Convention was held on:
A: 4 July 1776 | 19–20 July 1848 | 8 March 1910 | 26 August 1920
K: B | 19–20 July 1848, New York.

Q: The only African-American to sign the Declaration of Sentiments was:
A: Sojourner Truth | Frederick Douglass | W. E. B. Du Bois | Booker T. Washington
K: B | Frederick Douglass.

Q: The 19th Amendment, giving American women the vote, was ratified in:
A: 1869 | 1893 | 1920 | 1928
K: C | 1920 (Wyoming Territory had given women the vote in 1869).

Q: The first European country to give women both the vote and the right to stand was:
A: Britain | Finland | Norway | Sweden
K: B | Finland (1906).

Q: The "Cat and Mouse Act" (1913) concerned:
A: Women's property | Hunger-striking suffragettes | Child custody | Divorce
K: B | Released hunger strikers and re-arrested them.

Q: Equal voting rights at 21 came to British women in:
A: 1918 | 1920 | 1928 | 1945
K: C | 1928 (women over 30 with property voted from 1918).

Q: The Egyptian Feminist Union (1923) was founded by:
A: Huda Shaarawi | Qasim Amin | Nawal El Saadawi | None of these
K: A | Huda Shaarawi.

Q: The 1968 strike that led to Britain's Equal Pay Act 1970 was by women at:
A: Ford Dagenham | Rover Coventry | Ruskin College | Chiswick
K: A | Ford Dagenham machinists.

Q: The first modern women's shelter opened in 1971 in:
A: Toronto | Chiswick, London | New York | Stockholm
K: B | Chiswick, London.

Q: The opposition to the US Equal Rights Amendment was led by:
A: Gloria Steinem | Phyllis Schlafly | Betty Friedan | Bella Abzug
K: B | Phyllis Schlafly; the ERA was never ratified.

Q: Roe v. Wade was overturned in 2022 by:
A: *Planned Parenthood v. Casey* | *Dobbs v. Jackson* | *Griswold v. Connecticut* | None of these
K: B | *Dobbs v. Jackson Women's Health Organization* (2022).

Q: *Backlash* (1991), describing the counter-attack on feminism in the 1980s, was written by:
A: Naomi Wolf | Angela McRobbie | Rebecca Walker | None of these
K: D | None of these — Susan Faludi.

Q: The phrase "Me Too" was first used in 2006 by:
A: Alyssa Milano | Tarana Burke | Laura Bates | Anita Hill
K: B | Tarana Burke; #MeToo went global in October 2017.

Q: SlutWalk began in 2011 in:
A: London | Toronto | New Delhi | Buenos Aires
K: B | Toronto.

Q: #NiUnaMenos ("Not one less") began in 2015 in:
A: Mexico | Argentina | Spain | Chile
K: B | Argentina.

Q: The UN Commission on the Status of Women was set up in:
A: 1945 | 1946 | 1975 | 1979
K: B | 1946.

Q: The UN Decade for Women ran from:
A: 1975–1985 | 1976–1985 | 1980–1990 | 1985–1995
K: B | 1976–1985, after International Women's Year (1975).

Q: Which is correct about CEDAW?
A: It was adopted on 18 December 1979 | It entered into force on 3 September 1981 | Both A and B | None of these
K: C | Both: adopted 18 December 1979; in force 3 September 1981.

Q: CEDAW's Article 14 concerns:
A: Marriage and family | Rural women | Temporary special measures | Political life
K: B | Rural women (Art. 16 marriage; Art. 4 special measures; Art. 7 political life).

Q: Pakistan's reservation to CEDAW concerns Article:
A: 4 | 16 | 29(1) | None of these
K: C | A reservation to Article 29(1), plus a general declaration.

Q: CEDAW's Optional Protocol (1999), which Pakistan has not joined, allows:
A: Reservations | Individual complaints | Country visits only | Withdrawal
K: B | Individual complaints.

Q: The first head of UN Women (2010) was:
A: Phumzile Mlambo-Ngcuka | Michelle Bachelet | Sima Bahous | Emma Watson
K: B | Michelle Bachelet.

Q: The Muslim League formed its Central Women's Sub-Committee in 1938 at:
A: Lahore | Patna | Aligarh | Karachi
K: B | Patna.

Q: The only Muslim woman delegate at the Round Table Conferences was:
A: Fatima Jinnah | Begum Jahanara Shahnawaz | Begum Ra'ana Liaquat Ali Khan | Begum Shaista Ikramullah
K: B | Begum Jahanara Shahnawaz.

Q: Who hoisted the Muslim League flag on the Punjab Civil Secretariat in 1947?
A: Fatima Sughra | Fatima Jinnah | Salma Tasadduq Hussain | None of these
K: A | Fatima Sughra.

Q: PM Bogra's second marriage (1955) led to the commission on marriage and family laws headed by:
A: Justice Rashid | Justice Munir | Justice Cornelius | None of these
K: A | The Rashid Commission, which led to the MFLO 1961.

Q: The Women's Action Forum was founded in Karachi in 1981 after the case of:
A: Safia Bibi | Fehmida and Allah Bux | Saima Waheed | Mukhtaran Mai
K: B | The Fehmida–Allah Bux case.

Q: 12 February became Pakistan's National Women's Day in:
A: 1983 | 2002 | 2012 | 2018
K: C | 2012, commemorating the Lahore protest of 12 February 1983.

Q: The mass movement of rural Sindhi women from 1982 was the:
A: Sindhiani Tehreek | Aurat March | Lady Health Workers' movement | None of these
K: A | Sindhiani Tehreek.

Q: Al-Huda was founded in 1994 by:
A: Farhat Hashmi | Asma Jahangir | Hina Jilani | Riffat Hassan
K: A | Farhat Hashmi.

Q: Writers on the "NGO-isation" of women's movements include:
A: Sonia Alvarez and Islah Jad | Moser and Molyneux | Bowles and Klein | None of these
K: A | Sonia Alvarez (1999) and Islah Jad (2004).
