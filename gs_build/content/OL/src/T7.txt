Q: The UN Declaration on the Elimination of Violence against Women (DEVAW) was adopted on:
A: 18 December 1979 | 20 December 1993 | 4 September 1995 | 25 November 1999
K: B | 20 December 1993.

Q: DEVAW Article 2 names three sites of violence. Which is NOT one of them?
A: The family | The community | The State | The market
K: D | The family, the community and the State.

Q: CEDAW General Recommendation 19 (1992) declared gender-based violence a form of:
A: Crime | Discrimination | Torture | None of these
K: B | Discrimination; updated by GR 35 (2017).

Q: In the Beijing Platform, violence against women is Critical Area:
A: A | B | D | G
K: C | Critical Area D.

Q: The International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women is:
A: 8 March | 25 November | 10 December | 12 February
K: B | 25 November, start of the 16 Days of Activism ending on 10 December.

Q: The Istanbul Convention on violence against women was adopted by the Council of Europe in:
A: 1993 | 2006 | 2011 | 2017
K: C | 2011.

Q: SDG target 5.3 calls for eliminating:
A: All violence against women | Child, early and forced marriage | Unpaid care work | None of these
K: B | Child, early and forced marriage (5.2 is all violence).

Q: About how many women and girls were killed by partners or family members in 2023 (UNODC and UN Women)?
A: 5,100 | 21,000 | 51,100 | 85,000
K: C | About 51,100 — one every 10 minutes (85,000 killed intentionally in all).

Q: Violence against women as a "continuum" (1988) was described by:
A: Liz Kelly | Lenore Walker | Evan Stark | Lori Heise
K: A | Liz Kelly.

Q: Bandura's Bobo doll experiments (1961) showed that children:
A: Are born aggressive | Copy aggression they observe | Never copy adults | None of these
K: B | Children imitate observed aggression.

Q: The resource theory of family violence (1971) was proposed by:
A: William Goode | Murray Straus | Albert Bandura | Johan Galtung
K: A | William Goode.

Q: *Violence Against Wives* (1979) was written by:
A: Dobash and Dobash | Pence and Paymar | Thaddeus and Maine | None of these
K: A | R. Emerson Dobash and Russell Dobash.

Q: *Against Our Will* (1975), arguing that rape is about power, was written by:
A: Andrea Dworkin | Susan Brownmiller | Catharine MacKinnon | Kate Millett
K: B | Susan Brownmiller.

Q: The "cycle of violence" and "learned helplessness" were described in *The Battered Woman* (1979) by:
A: Lenore Walker | Liz Kelly | Ellen Pence | Evan Stark
K: A | Lenore Walker.

Q: The Power and Control Wheel was developed in:
A: Toronto | Duluth, Minnesota | Chiswick, London | Islamabad
K: B | Duluth, by the Domestic Abuse Intervention Project (from 1981).

Q: *Coercive Control* (2007) was written by:
A: Michael Johnson | Michael Kimmel | Michael Kaufman | None of these
K: D | None of these — Evan Stark.

Q: The distinction between "intimate terrorism" and "situational couple violence" was drawn by:
A: Michael Johnson | Michael Kaufman | Michael Kimmel | None of these
K: A | Michael Johnson.

Q: Lori Heise's ecological model (1998) has four levels. Which is NOT one of them?
A: Individual | Relationship | Community | Global
K: D | Individual, relationship, community and society.

Q: "Structural violence" (1969) was defined by:
A: Johan Galtung | Lori Heise | R. W. Connell | Liz Kelly
K: A | Johan Galtung, who added "cultural violence" in 1990.

Q: Kaufman's "triad of men's violence" (1987) includes violence against women, against other men and against:
A: Children | Themselves | The State | Property
K: B | Themselves.

Q: "Aggrieved entitlement" is a term of:
A: Michael Kimmel | Michael Kaufman | R. W. Connell | Evan Stark
K: A | Michael Kimmel.

Q: The White Ribbon Campaign of men against violence began in 1991 in:
A: Britain | Canada | Pakistan | Australia
K: B | Canada.

Q: UN Women's HeForShe campaign was launched in:
A: 2010 | 2014 | 2017 | 2021
K: B | 2014.

Q: The share of ever-married women in Pakistan who experienced physical violence since age 15 (PDHS 2017–18) was:
A: 18% | 28% | 34% | 56%
K: B | 28% (34% spousal violence of any kind; 56% never sought help).

Q: *Siyahkari* is the term for honour killing in:
A: Sindhi | Balochi | Pashto | Punjabi
K: B | Balochi (*karo-kari* Sindhi, *tor tora* Pashto, *kala kali* Punjabi).

Q: Exchange marriage between two families is called:
A: *Vani* | *Swara* | *Watta satta* | *Haq bakhshish*
K: C | *Watta satta*.

Q: The Qisas and Diyat Ordinance (1990) allowed victims' heirs to:
A: Appeal to the FSC | Forgive the killer | Demand the death penalty only | None of these
K: B | Forgive the killer — the loophole later restricted in 2016.

Q: The Acid Control and Acid Crime Prevention Act 2011 added which sections to the Penal Code?
A: 310A and 310B | 336A and 336B | 498A and 498B | 509 and 509A
K: B | Sections 336A and 336B.

Q: Section 310A PPC punishes:
A: Forced marriage | Giving a woman in *vani* or *swara* | Marriage to the Qur'an | Denial of inheritance
K: B | *Vani*/*swara* (498A inheritance, 498B forced marriage, 498C marriage to the Qur'an).

Q: The first provincial domestic-violence law in Pakistan was passed in 2013 by:
A: Punjab | Sindh | Balochistan | Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
K: B | Sindh (Balochistan 2014, Punjab 2016, KP 2021, Islamabad 2025).

Q: The Anti-Rape (Investigation and Trial) Act received assent on:
A: 24 February 2016 | 1 December 2021 | 9 October 2012 | None of these
K: B | 1 December 2021.

Q: The Punjab Protection of Women against Violence Act 2016 requires the Family Court to decide complaints within:
A: 30 days | 60 days | 90 days | 6 months
K: C | 90 days.

Q: Under the Punjab Act 2016, a protection order may require the offender to wear:
A: A GPS bracelet | A uniform | A badge | None of these
K: A | A GPS ankle or wrist bracelet in cases of grave violence.

Q: The Punjab Protection of Women against Violence Act 2016 created:
A: District Women Protection Committees | Women Protection Officers | Both A and B | None of these
K: C | Both.

Q: The first women's police stations in Pakistan opened in:
A: 1988 | 1994 | 2002 | 2017
K: B | 1994.

Q: Mukhtaran Mai's memoir is titled:
A: *I Am Malala* | *In the Name of Honor* | *Saving Face* | None of these
K: B | *In the Name of Honor* (2006).

Q: Malala Yousafzai's BBC Urdu diary (2009) was written under the name:
A: Gul Makai | Saba Qaiser | Zainab | None of these
K: A | "Gul Makai".

Q: Sharmeen Obaid-Chinoy's Oscar-winning *Saving Face* (2012) is about:
A: Honour killing | Acid attacks | Child marriage | Girls' education
K: B | Acid attacks; co-directed with Daniel Junge.
