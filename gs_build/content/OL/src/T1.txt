Q: Who first used the term "gender role", in 1955?
A: Robert Stoller | John Money | Ann Oakley | Margaret Mead
K: B | John Money introduced "gender role" in 1955; Stoller (1968) and Oakley (1972) came later.

Q: *Sex and Gender* (1968), which separated biological sex from gender identity, was written by:
A: Robert Stoller | Ann Oakley | Gayle Rubin | Judith Lorber
K: A | Robert Stoller, *Sex and Gender* (1968).

Q: Ann Oakley brought the sex/gender distinction into sociology in:
A: *The Second Sex* (1949) | *Sex, Gender and Society* (1972) | *Gender Trouble* (1990) | *Paradoxes of Gender* (1994)
K: B | Oakley, *Sex, Gender and Society* (1972).

Q: Margaret Mead's *Sex and Temperament* (1935) studied three societies of:
A: Samoa | New Guinea | Borneo | None of these
K: B | The Arapesh, Mundugumor and Tchambuli of New Guinea.

Q: "Is Female to Male as Nature Is to Culture?" (1974) was written by:
A: Michelle Rosaldo | Gayle Rubin | Sherry Ortner | Carol Gilligan
K: C | Sherry Ortner (1974).

Q: The "sex/gender system" was described by Gayle Rubin in:
A: "The Traffic in Women" (1975) | "Doing Gender" (1987) | "The Five Sexes" (1993) | None of these
K: A | Rubin, "The Traffic in Women" (1975).

Q: One of the first Women's Studies courses was taught in 1969 at:
A: Harvard University | Cornell University | San Diego State College | Oxford University
K: B | Cornell (1969); the first full programme was at San Diego State College (1970).

Q: The US National Women's Studies Association was founded in:
A: 1969 | 1970 | 1977 | 1983
K: C | NWSA, 1977.

Q: The autonomy/integration debate was framed in *Theories of Women's Studies* (1983) by:
A: Bowles and Klein | Mumtaz and Shaheed | West and Zimmerman | Berger and Luckmann
K: A | Gloria Bowles and Renate Duelli Klein (1983).

Q: Pakistan's Women's Studies centres were set up in five public universities in:
A: 1979 | 1986 | 1989 | 2002
K: C | 1989, by the federal Ministry of Women's Development.

Q: Which of the following was NOT one of the five universities given a Women's Studies centre in 1989?
A: University of Karachi | University of Peshawar | University of Sindh | University of Balochistan
K: C | The five: Quaid-i-Azam, Karachi, Punjab, Peshawar and Balochistan.

Q: *Women of Pakistan: Two Steps Forward, One Step Back?* (1987) was written by:
A: Rubina Saigol and Farzana Bari | Khawar Mumtaz and Farida Shaheed | Kamla Bhasin and Nighat Said Khan | None of these
K: B | Khawar Mumtaz and Farida Shaheed (1987).

Q: The early, weak method of simply adding women to old subjects is described as:
A: Mainstreaming | "Add women and stir" | Gynocriticism | Praxis
K: B | "Add women and stir" — adding women without changing frameworks.

Q: Joining study with action, a key feature of Gender Studies, is called:
A: Praxis | Androcentrism | Essentialism | Deconstruction
K: A | Praxis.

Q: *Language and Woman's Place* (1975) was written by:
A: Dale Spender | Deborah Tannen | Robin Lakoff | Muriel Schulz
K: C | Robin Lakoff (1975).

Q: *Man Made Language* (1980), arguing that men control the meanings of words, was written by:
A: Dale Spender | Robin Lakoff | Laura Mulvey | Judith Lorber
K: A | Dale Spender (1980).

Q: Deborah Tannen (1990) contrasted women's talk with men's "report talk". She called women's style:
A: Small talk | Rapport talk | Hedged talk | Tag talk
K: B | "Rapport talk" versus "report talk".

Q: The pairs master/mistress and bachelor/spinster illustrate:
A: The generic masculine | Semantic derogation | Marked terms | Performativity
K: B | Muriel Schulz's "semantic derogation" (1975).

Q: The term "male gaze" was coined by:
A: Erving Goffman | Laura Mulvey | Michel Foucault | Elaine Showalter
K: B | Laura Mulvey (1975).

Q: The term "queer theory" was coined (1990–91) by:
A: Judith Butler | Eve Kosofsky Sedgwick | Teresa de Lauretis | None of these
K: C | Teresa de Lauretis.

Q: *Epistemology of the Closet* (1990) was written by:
A: Eve Kosofsky Sedgwick | Teresa de Lauretis | Adrienne Rich | Anne Fausto-Sterling
K: A | Eve Kosofsky Sedgwick (1990).

Q: Thomas Laqueur's *Making Sex* (1990) described Europe's shift:
A: From two sexes to one | From a one-sex to a two-sex model | From nature to culture | None of these
K: B | One-sex to two-sex model.

Q: "The Five Sexes" (1993) was written by:
A: Anne Fausto-Sterling | Cordelia Fine | Daphna Joel | Sandra Bem
K: A | Anne Fausto-Sterling.

Q: In Kohlberg's theory, "gender constancy" is reached at about age:
A: 2–3 | 4–5 | 6–7 | 10–12
K: C | Identity 2–3, stability 4–5, constancy 6–7.

Q: Gender schema theory (1981) was proposed by:
A: Lawrence Kohlberg | Albert Bandura | Walter Mischel | None of these
K: D | None of these — Sandra Bem (1981).

Q: Harold Garfinkel's study of "Agnes" (1967) showed that gender is:
A: Biologically fixed | An ongoing accomplishment | A grammatical category | A legal status
K: B | Gender as an ongoing accomplishment in everyday life.

Q: Judith Lorber's *Paradoxes of Gender* (1994) described gender as:
A: A performance | A social institution | A hormone effect | A schema
K: B | Gender as a social institution.

Q: Classical Islamic law recognised the intersex person as:
A: *Khawaja sira* | *Khuntha* | *Mukhannath* | None of these
K: B | *Khuntha*.

Q: The 2017 Census counted how many transgender persons in Pakistan?
A: 1,041 | 10,418 | 104,180 | 1,041,800
K: B | 10,418 (activists say a large undercount).

Q: The Federal Shariat Court held parts of the Transgender Persons Act against Islamic injunctions on:
A: 19 May 2023 | 9 October 2018 | 25 December 2009 | None of these
K: A | 19 May 2023; appeals remained pending in 2026.

Q: Carol Gilligan's *In a Different Voice* (1982) found in girls an:
A: Ethic of justice | Ethic of care | Ethic of honour | Ethic of duty
K: B | An "ethic of care", undervalued by older theories.

Q: India's report that sparked Women's Studies in South Asia, *Towards Equality*, appeared in:
A: 1969 | 1974 | 1985 | 1995
K: B | 1974.
